Assign the B2B Policy Administrator role to users who need to do the following tasks:

- Create, read, update, and delete B2B management policies.
- Configure invitation domain restrictions and automatic invitation redemption in B2B management policies.
- Set the tenant-default B2B management policy.

This role applies to tenant-wide B2B management policies. It doesn't grant administration of other policy types, cross-tenant access policies, users, applications, or role assignments.
